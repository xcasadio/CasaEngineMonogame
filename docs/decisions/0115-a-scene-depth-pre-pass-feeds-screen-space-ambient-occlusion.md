# ADR-0115: A scene depth pre-pass feeds screen-space ambient occlusion

- **Status**: Proposed
- **Date**: 2026-11-15
- **Source**: this chantier: `ai-agent/tasks/pbr-clearcoat-ssao-fxaa-tasks.md` (arbitrations P1, P6 and P7, tasks T3.1 and T3.2; the occlusion and its composition, T3.3, complete this record)

## Context

ADR-0112 extends the PBR scope to screen-space ambient occlusion (SSAO). SSAO needs the depth of the scene as a texture, and nothing provides it: the depth buffer of the HDR scene target is not sampleable, no linear depth or normal target exists, and no shader reads the screen position. A forward renderer without a depth pre-pass has to draw its opaque geometry once more to get it, in a target of its own.

T3.1 checked the plan's six architecture decisions against the code and measured, on the test device (Intel UHD Graphics, MonoGame DesktopGL 3.8.5.1, `ps_3_0`):

- The renderers draw one after the other in `RenderSceneCore`: static, skinned, sprites, particles, 3D lines. The opaque scene geometry is the static and the skinned meshes only (there is no terrain component). The static renderer draws its transparent items before the skinned renderer draws anything, so no point exists "after all the opaque geometry and before all the transparent geometry": the composition goes between `RenderScene` and `ApplyBloom` (second case of P6, written in T3.3).
- Pooled render targets are `PreserveContents`: a target keeps its colour **and** its depth buffer when another target is bound and the first one comes back, with no clear (1984 of 1984 pixels kept, for `Single`/`Depth24` and for `HalfVector4`/`Depth24Stencil8`).
- `Single`, `HalfSingle`, `HalfVector4` and `Vector4` targets can be created, cleared to 60 000 exactly, and written with the clip-space w of a perspective projection (the distance along the camera axis): worst error 0.0013 % in `Single`, 0.087 % in the half formats.
- A `Single` target is read back by a `tex2D` exactly as by the CPU, with no vertical flip.

## Decision

- **A host owned by the pipeline.** `RenderPipeline` owns one `SceneDepthPrepass`, exposed to the renderers like the IBL cache: `RenderFrame.SceneDepth` and `RenderContext.SceneDepth`, set with the frame in `Render`. The object holds the state of the view being rendered (its target, the saved device binding); it is shared by all the views, which render one after the other (split screen, render to texture), so its counters are not kept on it: each view counts in its own `RenderStats`.
- **Four activation conditions, evaluated in `RenderHdrView` before the scene is rendered.** The pre-pass is active for a view only when (1) `SsaoEnabled` is set (`ssao_enabled`, off by default), (2) the view renders through the HDR target (`LinearHdr`; `RenderHdrView` is where it is decided), (3) its projection is perspective (`frame.Projection.M44 == 0`: the depth written is the clip-space w), and (4) its pipeline separates the scene from the interface (`pipeline is IScenePhaseViewPipeline` with `SupportsScenePhase`, the `composeInterfaceAfter` condition of the FXAA pass). Condition 4 matters because a pipeline that renders everything at once, the editor's `OverlayViewPipeline`, draws the grid, the handles and the interface into the HDR target: an effect that multiplies that target would darken them, exactly as FXAA would blur them (ADR-0113). **Limit: a view without a scene phase, and a view with an orthographic projection, have no pre-pass and no SSAO**, written next to FXAA's limit. T3.3's composition runs only when the pre-pass is active for the view and `Ssao.fx` is loaded.
- **Each renderer draws its own depth, in its own `Flush`.** The host binds its target (`BeginDraw`), the renderer draws, `EndDraw` puts the previous target back, with no clear: because the pooled targets preserve their contents, both renderers write into one colour and one depth buffer. Static: a `SceneDepthPass` inserted in `ForwardRenderPipeline` right after the shadow pass (before the sky), with its own `RenderPassType` member `SceneDepthPass` appended to the enumeration (the existing `DepthPrePass` member is documented to write the depth buffer only and is used by nobody; it is left as it is). Skinned: `SkinnedMeshRendererComponent.DrawSceneDepth`, after the shadow casters and before the colour draws, with the `RiggedModelSceneDepth` and `RiggedModelSceneDepthDualQuaternion` techniques of `skinEffect.fx`. The `PostShadowPassCallback` slot is not reused: it holds one delegate and the skinned shadows already own it.
- **Output.** The view depth in world units (the clip-space w), **not normalised**: no near or far plane is needed to read it back, and a reader reconstructs the view position from the projection (`M11`, `M22`). The target is cleared to `SceneDepthPrepass.BackgroundDepth` = **60 000** (exact in every candidate format, the largest `Half` being 65 504), and a reader treats any depth at or beyond `SceneDepthPrepass.SkyThreshold` = **30 000** as sky. **Limit: a camera far plane is unbounded, and geometry farther than 30 000 units from the camera plane reads as sky.** The far plane is not deduced from the matrix (`M43 / (M33 + 1)` loses about 0.1 % of precision at 5000 in `float`).
- **Format.** `Single` with `DepthFormat.Depth24`, falling back once, logged, to `HalfSingle` then `HalfVector4`; when none can be created the pre-pass is switched off with one warning (as the bloom does for a `Color` HDR format). The format of the depth buffer, `Depth24` (never `Depth24Stencil8`), is also what tells the target apart when it is released.
- **What is drawn, and the F1 invariant.** The static pass walks the sorted list like `OpaquePass`: items of a queue below `Transparent`, with their buffers, instanced items skipped. It deliberately does **not** reuse the shadow filter (`EffectiveCastShadows`): an object that casts no shadow still occludes. The invariant, pinned by a GPU test that runs the real `OpaquePass`: `RenderStats.SceneDepthStaticDraws` equals `RenderStats.OpaqueItems` of the same flush. The instanced path is unreachable at run time today (no code produces `ShaderFeature.Instanced`), so the measured instance count is 0; should it be enabled, the pre-pass must follow what the colour pass draws.
- **Alpha cut-out (F3).** An item with a `ShadowMaskTexture` (the alpha-tested queue) uses the textured technique and the material cutoff on the alpha of the texture alone, **the approximation of the shadow pass**; the colour pass cuts on the product of the alpha factor and the texture alpha, so the two differ when the factor is below 1 (limit). A skinned mesh cuts with `mesh.AlphaCutoff` when it is 0 or more, else with the material cutoff when the material is alpha tested, else not at all, the rule the colour pass ends up with; it writes the depth of semi-transparent textures without a cutoff, as the colour pass does.
- **No per-frame allocation (F6).** `GraphicsStateGuard` allocates an array at every capture, which `ShadowPass` and the skinned shadows already accept; the pre-pass does not add to it: `SceneDepthPrepass` keeps one `RenderTargetBinding[1]`, filled by `GraphicsDevice.GetRenderTargets(RenderTargetBinding[])` and given back by `SetRenderTargets`, and saves and restores the viewport, the scissor rectangle and the blend, depth, rasterizer and first sampler states by value. A call finding anything other than exactly one bound target draws nothing.
- **Eviction (rule "Cibles mises en commun").** The key of the target (width, height, format, `Depth24`) is stamped in `_hdrLastUsedRender` with the render index when it is acquired; `ReleaseUnusedHdrTargets` has a branch for the keys with a `Depth24` buffer, before the FXAA branch (`Color`, no depth) and the bloom branch (no depth), counting in `SceneDepthTargetCreationCount` and `SceneDepthTargetDisposalCount`, which the bloom and FXAA counters do not include. **Note: the shadow atlas has the same key (`Single`, `Depth24`) when its resolution equals the view size.** An acquired target is in no free queue and the sweep only trims free ones, so a live atlas is never disposed; a freed atlas of that size would be counted as a pre-pass disposal.
- **Proof of execution.** `RenderStats.SceneDepthStaticDraws` and `SceneDepthSkinnedDraws` count the draws of the pre-pass per view and per frame (reset with the other counters); the pipeline counts its activations in `SceneDepthPrepassCount`; the demo harness writes both per view once, at capture time (`[DemosGame] Scene depth draws at capture`).
- **Settings.** `ColorPipelineSettings.SsaoEnabled`, `SsaoRadius` (0.5), `SsaoIntensity` (1) and `SsaoBias` (0.02), keys `ssao_enabled`, `ssao_radius`, `ssao_intensity`, `ssao_bias`, additive like the bloom ones, copied by the resolver, edited in the details panel and driven in demos by `CASAENGINE_DEMO_SSAO`, `_RADIUS`, `_INTENSITY` and `_BIAS`. T3.2 only uses the enable flag; the others belong to the occlusion pass.
- **Limits of the occlusion** (P6, P7; written here for the whole feature): the transparent geometry and the other 3D draws (particles, 3D lines, sprites, tile maps) are not in the depth, but the multiplication that T3.3 adds applies to everything already in the HDR target, so they are darkened by the occlusion of what is behind them; the occlusion multiplies all of the opaque colour, direct light included.

## Consequences

- With SSAO off, nothing changes: no target is acquired, no draw is added, and the render of every demo is bit-identical (the non-regression fingerprints of T0.1 are unchanged).
- With SSAO on, the opaque static and skinned geometry is drawn once more, with a minimal shader (one texture read per pixel for a cut-out only), into a 4-byte-per-pixel `Single` target plus a 24-bit depth buffer per view size. T4.1 measures the cost.
- The pre-pass has no visual effect by itself: until the occlusion pass exists, an image rendered with SSAO on is the same as without.
- The skinned renderer's second pass over its meshes duplicates the pose and vertex-buffer selection of the colour draw; a change in the skinned colour draw (a mesh it would skip) must be mirrored in `DrawSceneDepth` (a text test pins the order, a GPU test pins the result).
- Hot reload: `SceneDepth.fx` is a built-in shader reloaded by the static renderer like `ShadowDepth.fx`; the skinned techniques reload with `skinEffect.fx`.
